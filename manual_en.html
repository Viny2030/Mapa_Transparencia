1. What the ecosystem is

The Transparency Ecosystem is a set of 12 sites that monitor the Argentine State using official public data: public procurement, the Executive's staff roster, the budget, asset declarations, the Judiciary, Congress and international corruption cases. Each site works on its own, but many query each other: a supplier found in Contracts is checked against MEACI's sanctioned list; Decla's asset declarations are cross-checked against Contracts' awards; the IRI Monitor builds its index from Judiciary, Deputies, Senators and Contracts data.

This manual explains which layer each site belongs to, what it does, where its data comes from and what it connects to, so the whole ecosystem can be navigated and every figure traced back to its source.

All sites live under the ecosistematransparencia.com.ar domain (for example contratos.ecosistematransparencia.com.ar) and update automatically.

2. Layered architecture

Sites are arranged in layers according to how close they are to the original data. Lower layers pull directly from official sources; upper layers combine what others produce.

LayerSitesRole
PortalMapaIntegrates everything: links the sites, shows their live status and headline indicators. Runs the daily cross-validation.
Layer 1Comprar · Contratos · JefaturaProcurement and Executive sources. Ingest raw data (Official Gazette, COMPR.AR, Treasury, Mapa del Estado) and normalise it. The base every other layer builds on.
Layer 2IRI Monitor · MEACI · AjusteCross-cutting analysis. Combine sources into indices or reference lists: the Institutional Risk Index, the list of internationally sanctioned companies and the real-terms evolution of the budget.
Layer 3Decla · JusticiaAssets and Judiciary. Asset declarations of officials, legislators and judges, and the functioning of the Judiciary.
Layer 4Deputies · SenatorsLegislative branch. Composition, attendance, votes, productivity and cost of both chambers.
SatelliteVectoresResearch module on foreign state influence (strategic corruption). Runs separately and is planned to feed the IRI.

The layer numbering matches the site control and verification checklist, so the manual and the checklist can be read together.

comprar.ecosistematransparencia.com.ar

Every day it takes the Official Gazette's awards, cross-checks them with COMPR.AR and Treasury payments, and classifies decisions under the Corruptive Phenomena Theory (regressive income transfers).

6. How figures are verified

Two automated checks run every working day, independent of each site's own code:

Ecosystem cross-validation (Mapa)

A script (scripts/validar_ecosistema.py in the Mapa repository) queries the production APIs Mon–Fri at 13:00 ART and checks:

  1. Comprar ↔ Contratos: the same Official Gazette awards per day, with the same amount.
  2. Contratos, internal consistency: the summary total matches the sum by agency, and what share of awards carries an amount.
  3. MEACI ↔ Contratos and Comprar: all three sites recognise the same sanctioned-CUIT list, and whether those companies actually appear as suppliers.
  4. IRI Monitor: how many agencies have the procurement component and from which year that data is.
  5. Ajuste: the result of its own audit (below).
  6. Freshness: the data date of each site.

Each check ends as OK, warning or failure. Any failure automatically opens a GitHub issue; every run's report is in the repository's Actions tab.

Ajuste figure audit

Ajuste independently recomputes the deflator and average exchange rate from the Central Bank API, checks that the latest CPI is no older than 75 days, that the budget total does not drop more than 10% overnight (a sign of an incomplete file, not a cut) and that no regulation disappears. It runs at 10:30 ART.

First run (2026-11-05): 12 checks OK, 4 warnings, 0 failures. Comprar and Contratos matched to the peso from 2 to 4 November; the three sanctioned lists are identical; Ajuste's audit passed on recent days.

Manual verification

To review a site by hand: open it with Ctrl+F5, find the update date in the header or footer, check that indicator cards are not at 0 and tables have rows, and open the console (F12) to look for red errors.

7. Investigation walkthroughs

Examples of using several sites together to answer a question.

A · Who is this State supplier?

  1. Contratos: search the supplier by name or CUIT; see how much and with which agencies it contracted.
  2. Comprar: see the award in the Official Gazette and whether the Treasury has paid it.
  3. MEACI: check whether the CUIT belongs to a company sanctioned abroad.
  4. Decla: check whether any official declares a stake in that company (conflict-of-interest alert).

B · Is this official's wealth reasonable?

  1. Decla: open the profile (Ver): evolution in USD, IVPI, cash and offshore assets.
  2. Jefatura (if in the Executive): see their post and area in the roster.
  3. Contratos: check whether their companies received awards.

C · Which agency is riskiest, and why?

  1. Mapa / IRI Monitor: see the ranking and the heaviest component.
  2. Depending on the area: Justicia (vacancies, efficiency), Deputies or Senators (attendance, productivity) or Contratos (the agency's awards).
  3. Ajuste: see how its budget evolved in real terms.

8. Known limitations

Found by the cross-validation and the site checks of 4–5 November 2026:

  • MEACI alerts: alerts tagged contratos_v2 mean the sanctioned company has a presence in Argentina; none of the 13 sanctioned CUITs currently appears as a supplier in Contratos.
  • Contratos total amount: only part of the awards report an amount, so the peso total is a floor, not the total awarded.
  • IRI procurement risk: computed for 25 of 82 agencies, from COMPR.AR 2020-2021 data.
  • Different schedules: Comprar processes the Gazette at 08:30 and Contratos at 09:30; in between, Contratos does not yet show the day's awards. The cross-validation runs at 13:00, once both have finished.
  • Planned integrations: MEACI and Vectores do not feed the IRI yet.

9. Glossary

BORAOfficial Gazette of the Argentine Republic. Its 3rd section publishes procurement notices and awards.
COMPR.ARThe national public administration's electronic procurement system.
TGNNational Treasury: records actual payments to suppliers.
CUITArgentine tax identification number; identifies companies and people in cross-checks.
IRIInstitutional Risk Index: 0–100 score per agency combining financial, procurement, operational and data risk.
IVPIUnjustified Wealth Variation Index: how many times the wealth increase exceeds declared income. Does not imply wrongdoing.
MJRMultijurisdictional resolution: a foreign-bribery case resolved in more than one country (OECD report).
DA / DNUAdministrative decision / Decree of necessity and urgency; instruments that modify the budget.
OAAnti-Corruption Office: publishes asset declarations.
HCDNNational Chamber of Deputies.
XAIExplainable AI: the system shows why it flags a risk.
NESTAnalytical framework for detecting and assessing strategic corruption by foreign states.

10. Legal framework and disclaimer

All data comes from official public sources. Its reuse is covered by Law 27,275 on Access to Public Information and Decree 1172/2003. Information on public officials in the exercise of their duties is processed in accordance with Law 25,326 and AAIP doctrine.

⚠️ Indicators, indices and alerts are algorithmic risk results for academic and transparency purposes. They imply no value judgement, accusation or determination of responsibility regarding any person, company or agency.
